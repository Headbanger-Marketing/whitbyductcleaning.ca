Questions About Your Whitby Request

How can I find out whether return-side leaks are included in Whitby?

If the return side may have gaps, ask how those connections will be inspected and whether the proposed work includes sealing them. A return leak can draw air from outside the intended return path, depending on its location. Confirm the material and method planned for each joint, and compare exclusions so that supply-side work is not confused with return-side sealing.

How can I compare proposals by equipment and scope for a Whitby property?

Identify rooftop units, make-up air equipment and duct runs that should be considered, and request itemized inclusions and exclusions. Ask how access or unusual configurations affect the price rather than relying on an hourly estimate without defined work. A site review and clear boundaries help compare proposals and reduce misunderstandings about what is included.

For air duct cleaning in Whitby, how should I ask for a supply-and-return cleaning plan?

Compare the proposed work with your system layout, including accessible supply and return branches. Ask how negative pressure will be maintained while individual runs are brushed, so loosened debris is collected rather than released into rooms. Share photos of unusual access points or registers beforehand; the number and arrangement of runs can affect the work required.

For dryer vent cleaning in Whitby, how should I inspect the full route before comparing quotes?

Trace the vent from the dryer to the exterior hood and photograph visible elbows, long sections or difficult access. If the route passes up through a ceiling or wall, ask how the scope covers those concealed sections. Lint restriction can vary along the run, so clearing only the connection behind the appliance may leave other sections unexamined.

What needs clarifying about whether the blower is removed before work in Whitby?

Ask whether the wheel and housing will be cleaned in place or whether the blower assembly will be removed for detailing. If removal is proposed, ask how the motor, assembly and balance will be checked during reinstallation. Photograph the equipment label and access area beforehand; model, configuration and clearance can change the scope.
